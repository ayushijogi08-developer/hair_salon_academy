# src/assets/images/academy/

Place all verified academy photo files here.
Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp

## What to place here:
- Students and trainers at work during hands-on training
- Classroom and theory sessions in the studio
- Student haircuts on live models
- Certificate handover and graduation moments

## File Naming Convention:
Name images descriptively using lowercase words separated by hyphens.
The site automatically detects all images in this folder and generates clean alt text from the filename:

Examples:
- students-scissor-technique.jpg  -> Alt: "Students Scissor Technique"
- educator-cutting-demo.jpg       -> Alt: "Educator Cutting Demo"
- live-salon-colour-training.jpg  -> Alt: "Live Salon Colour Training"
- academy-certificate-handover.jpg -> Alt: "Academy Certificate Handover"

## Photo Guidelines:
- Natural salon lighting or well-lit indoor portraits
- Aspect ratio: 4:5 or 3:4 portrait preferred, 16:9 landscape also supported in bento layout
- Recommended resolution: at least 1200px wide, under 800KB
